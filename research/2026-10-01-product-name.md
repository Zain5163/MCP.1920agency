# Product name: replacing "AdsPilot"

**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Why:** "AdsPilot" was a working name. The product is now the whole marketing
team (social posting, Google Business Profile, ads, SEO, analytics, website tags,
expert playbooks, approval gates), not just ads. Before the website and llms.txt
setup line get built, it needs a name that can be owned.

**Method:** web searches on this date for each name in marketing, SaaS and AI.
**Limits:** web search finds only what is indexed. It cannot show whether a
domain or npm name is free, and it is not a trademark search. Treat "no conflict
found" as "worth checking properly", not "clear".

---

## 1. Is "AdsPilot" already taken? Yes, many times over

| Who | What | How close |
|---|---|---|
| **adspilot.tech** | "AI-powered business growth": AI agents for Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, social media **and Google Business Profile** | **Almost the same product and the same pitch** |
| adspilot.dev | Facebook ads automation platform | Same field |
| ads-pilot.ai | AI Meta ads optimiser, "500+ advertisers", approve before push | Same field, approval story too |
| adspilotai.com | "Google Ads on autopilot" | Same field |
| github.com/adsorgcn/AdsPilot | Open-source Google Ads automation | Same name, same field |
| App Store "AdsPilot: ASA Campaign Tool" | Apple Search Ads tool | Same field |
| Crunchbase "AdsPilot" | Company profile | Exists |
| Near-identical: AdPilot (adpilot.ai, tryadpilot.com, myadpilot.com, pilotads.io, ad-pilot.ai, adpilot360, Niotex AdPilot) | All AI ad tools | Confusingly similar |

**Risk: high.** At least six live ad-software products use the exact name, and
adspilot.tech sells nearly the same bundle, GBP included. We could not register
it as a trademark, could not own search results for it, and could receive a
complaint once we have paying customers. The name also says "ads only", which
is wrong for the product. **Recommendation: drop it before anything public uses it.**

---

## 2. What a good name needs here

- Short, two or three syllables, and easy to say in **English and Urdu**.
- About the **whole marketing team**, not ads alone.
- Works as a domain (.com / .ai / .io) and as an npm / MCP package name
  (lowercase, no spaces: `npx <name>`).
- No obvious clash with an existing **marketing / SaaS / AI** product in the
  markets we sell to: UK, Europe, Gulf, Pakistan.

and they say something true about the founder and the South Asia-first angle.

## 4. Recommendation

### First choice: **Mahir**

- **One word that says what the product is for.** The pitch is "an expert
  marketing team inside your AI", and *mahir* means expert.
- **Easy in every market we sell to.** English speakers say it on first sight
  (MAH-heer). It is everyday Urdu, and Arabic speakers in the Gulf and Turkish
  speakers know it too.
- **Not ads-only.** It covers social, ads, SEO and analytics equally.
- **Works as a package and command**: `mahir`, `npx mahir`, `mahir-mcp`, and
  the setup line "add Mahir to your AI: https://…/llms.txt".
- **No marketing software or MCP found under the name.** The nearest is an
  Indonesian marketing-courses brand, which is a different class and market.
- **Risks to check:** (a) it is a common first name, so the exact .com is likely
  held; plan on mahir.ai, getmahir.com or mahirhq.com. (b) Because it means
  "expert", a trademark examiner could call it *descriptive* (in the US, foreign
  words are judged by their English meaning). A logo mark or a pairing such as
  "Mahir AI" may be needed. A trademark lawyer should confirm this before you
  commit.

### Second: **Hamkar**

"Co-worker", which sits well with "connect it to Claude and it works beside you".
It is the cleanest of the meaningful names, with no marketing software found, and
it sounds more distinctive in English, which makes the trademark easier. The
downside is that it is less familiar in everyday Urdu than *mahir*, and English
speakers may hear "ham car".

### Third: **Markwala**

No conflicts found anywhere. It is memorable and friendly, and very Pakistani
("chaiwala" is known in the UK too). The downside is that it may read as a local
or novelty brand to European buyers, and "Mark" can look like a person's name.

---

## 5. Before committing: the owner must do these

Nothing in this note replaces them.

1. **Trademark searches** in classes 9, 35 and 42 (software, advertising and
   marketing services, SaaS) at **USPTO** (US), **UKIPO** (UK), **EUIPO** (EU),
   **WIPO Global Brand Database** (wider, includes Gulf filings) and **IPO
   Pakistan**. Ask a lawyer about the descriptiveness risk for Mahir.
2. **Domain checks** at a registrar for .com, .ai and .io. Search cannot show
   availability. Decide whether a prefix (get-, use-, -hq) is acceptable.
3. **npm and GitHub**: run `npm view <name>` and check github.com/<name> and the
   MCP registries (Smithery, Glama, mcp.so) for the exact package name.
4. **Social handles** on Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube and TikTok.
5. Say it aloud to a few UK and Gulf clients, and check what it means in Arabic
   dialects and Hindi/Punjabi for anything unwanted.

Renaming is cheap now and expensive later. The name should be settled before
the website, llms.txt and any published npm package, since all of them carry it.
