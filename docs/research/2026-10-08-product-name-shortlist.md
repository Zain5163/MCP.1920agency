# Product name shortlist: replacing "AdsPilot" (round 2)

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Builds on:** `2026-10-01-product-name.md` (first choice was Mahir, then Hamkar, then
Markwala). This round re-checks those three with new tests and adds new candidates
for "many services, one AI operator / a marketing team inside your AI".

**What was checked, per name**

| Check | How | What it can and cannot tell you |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Companies and products | Web search, plus opening the live site | Finds only what is indexed |
| (b) Domains | RDAP registry lookups for .com, .ai, get&lt;name&gt;.com and &lt;name&gt;hq.com (via rdap.org), and for .io (Identity Digital registry RDAP), each tested against a known name and a made-up one. Registered sites were then opened | "Unregistered" means no registry record on 2026-10-08. The name is **probably** available, but it may be premium-priced. **Only a registrar can confirm.** "Registered" does not mean "not for sale" |
| (c) Trademarks | Trademarkia search, which shows US (USPTO) filings. It was tested on "hubspot" and "factotum" and returned their known marks | US only. UKIPO, EUIPO, WIPO and IPO Pakistan were **not** searched (their search tools need a browser). Zero results does not make a name clear |
| (d) Meaning and sound | English and Urdu, plus Arabic, Persian and Hindi where relevant | Not tested on native listeners yet |
| (e) CEO credibility | Judgement | Opinion |
| Package names | npm registry and GitHub handle lookups | Shows only whether the name exists right now |

Nothing was registered and no one was contacted.

---

## Recommendation

1. **Hamkar** (ہمکار, "co-worker"): the best balance of meaning and availability.
   "Your AI's marketing co-worker" describes the product, and it has no marketing
   or AI software rival in English-language markets. No US trademark was found. hamkar.io, hamkar.co,
   gethamkar.com and hamkarhq.com showed no registration, and hamkar.com is held by a
   domain investor who says it "may be for sale".
2. **Mahir** (ماہر, "expert"): still the clearest meaning, but this round found a new
   problem. **In Pakistan and the Gulf, "Mahir" already means a handyman app**: Mahir
   Company (Lahore, since 2019), GetMahir (UAE) and Mahir (Oman). The .com, .ai, .io and
   get-mahir.com domains are all taken. Use it only if the main market is UK, EU or US,
   with mahirhq.com.
3. **Markwala** ("the marketing one"): the cleanest result of every name checked.
   No company, no US trademark, and the .ai, .io, get- and -hq domains plus the npm and
   GitHub names all showed no registration. The cost is that it sounds local or playful,
   and a Pakistani ear may hear "marks wala" (exam marks).

**Reserve:** **Intezam** (انتظام, "management, arrangements"). It came back clean, with
no US trademark, an unregistered .ai and .io, and a .com listed for sale at GoDaddy.
But it sounds bureaucratic in Urdu and is hard for English speakers to say.

**What changed since 2026-10-01:** Hamkar and Mahir swapped places because of the
Mahir home-services brands in Pakistan and the Gulf. Every new English name checked
(Manyhands, Majordomo, Righthand, Factotum, Deckhand, Bosun, Opsmith) is already used by
an AI, automation or marketing business, or has weak domains. That confirms the
earlier finding: English "team / crew / hand" names are used up.

## Notes on the top three

**Hamkar.** The pitch is "connect it to Claude and it works beside you", and
*hamkar* means "the one who works beside you". It is also the only top pick with a
realistic route to a .com, since the current holder parks it for sale (price unknown).

- **Watch hamkar.ai.** Its registration ends on 2026-10-19. It may be renewed or
  auctioned, so do not count on it.
- **The Iranian link.** The Persian word is common in Iran, and Iran's largest software
  company trades as *Hamkaran System*. That business is confined to Iran by sanctions,
  but a Gulf or UK customer who searches the name will see Persian results.
- **Try it aloud.** Ask a few UK and Gulf clients to say it, to check whether
  "ham car" is a real problem.

**Mahir.** Last round said "no marketing software found", and that still holds. The
new finding is about **search and brand recall**:

- A Pakistani SMB owner who searches "Mahir app" lands on MrMahir.com, the
  home-services app, which has been well known since 2019.
- In the UAE and Oman, it is a tradesmen marketplace.

Different goods mean the trademark risk is low. The confusion risk in the owner's
home market and the Gulf is real, though. Add the descriptiveness risk from last
round and the fact that every main domain is taken, and it drops to second.

**Markwala.** It is the cleanest of all, and it would fit the owner's "Before It Has a
Name" newsletter, which tells the naming story. It suits a South Asia-first brand. For
a $9 product sold to UK dentists and Gulf real-estate firms, Hamkar or Mahir will sound
more like software.

## Before committing (unchanged from round 1, still required)

1. **Registrar check.** Search .com, .ai, .io and .co at a registrar for the chosen name.
   For hamkar.com, ask the price through a broker without giving the owner's name. Nothing
   was contacted in this research.
2. **Trademark search beyond the US**, in Classes 9, 35 and 42: UKIPO, EUIPO, **WIPO
   Global Brand Database** (which covers Gulf and Iranian filings) and **IPO Pakistan**.
   Ask a lawyer about the descriptiveness risk for Mahir.
3. **npm, GitHub and the MCP registries** (Smithery, Glama, mcp.so), plus social
   handles on Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok.
4. **Say it aloud** to UK and Gulf clients, and to Hindi, Punjabi and Persian speakers.
