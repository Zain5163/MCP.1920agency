# New Zealand

**Updated 2026-10-08.** Practical guidance, not legal advice: confirm anything binding
with a New Zealand lawyer. **V** = read on the official page on 2026-10-08; **R** =
reported by a secondary source or search summary; **Est.** = vendor data.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Currency | New Zealand dollar, $12.99 (NZ$ or NZD when selling across borders) |
| Price display | Prices to consumers **include GST (15%)** (V) |
| Returns | Consumer Guarantees Act remedies; no change-of-mind right; no "no refunds" wording |
| Language | New Zealand English; te reo Māori greetings are common, not required |
| How people pay | Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, Afterpay, bank transfer and POLi |
| Regulators | Commerce Commission (Fair Trading Act, CGA), Privacy Commissioner, DIA (spam) |

## How people pay, and what to show at checkout

- Show card logos, wallets, and Afterpay where offered. Shopify Payments and WooPayments
  are available to New Zealand merchants (V).
- POLi (bank payments) continues in New Zealand (it closed in Australia in 2023) (R).
  Laybuy went into receivership in June 2024 (R): remove old Laybuy badges.
- BNPL counts as consumer credit since 2 Sept 2024 (R); the provider carries those duties.
- No official payment-mix data was found; ask the owner what their buyers use.

## Prices, tax and discounts

- **GST-inclusive prices:** the Commerce Commission says consumers are entitled to assume
  the price shown is the full price, and fine print saying GST is extra is unlikely to
  stop them being misled (V). Show prices with GST included.
- **Was/now:** the "was" price must be the usual selling price; made-up, stale or rarely
  charged prices mislead, and a "sale" price used routinely becomes the usual price (R,
  Commerce Commission; a retailer was fined $780,000).
- Claims must have reasonable grounds when made (unsubstantiated representations) (V).

## Consumer law that changes pages

- **Consumer Guarantees Act 1993:** goods must be of acceptable quality, fit for purpose,
  match their description, be reasonably priced, and be delivered on time and in good
  condition. Minor failure: the business repairs, replaces or refunds. Substantial
  failure: the buyer may reject for a refund, or keep the goods and claim compensation (V).
- No change-of-mind right; a store may offer one as policy (V).
- Businesses should not say they give no refunds, or put conditions on refunds the law
  does not allow; doing so breaches the Fair Trading Act. Contracting out is allowed only
  between businesses, in writing (V).
- **Fair Trading Act 1986:** no misleading conduct about price, quality or discounts;
  online traders must say they are traders; unfair contract terms in standard-form
  consumer contracts can be struck down; extended warranties need a written agreement
  comparing them with CGA rights, and a 5-working-day cancellation (V).
- **Pending:** a Fair Trading Amendment Bill would raise maximum penalties (to the greater
  of NZ$5 million for companies or 3× the gain); select committee report due
  27 Nov 2026 (R).

## Privacy, cookies and tracking

- **Privacy Act 2020** information privacy principles apply to personal information
  gathered by forms, cookies and pixels. **IPP 3A (from 1 May 2026):** when you collect
  personal information about someone from another source, tell them, as soon as
  practicable, what was collected, why, who gets it, your name and address, and their
  access and correction rights (with exceptions) (V).
- No dedicated cookie-consent law was found (negative finding, not confirmed by a
  regulator). Say in the privacy policy which tracking is used and why.
- **Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007:** consent, sender identification, and a free
  unsubscribe honoured within 5 working days (V).

## Accessibility

- The government Web Accessibility Standard (WCAG 2.2 AA) applies to public agencies,
  not private businesses (R). The Human Rights Act 1993 prohibits disability
  discrimination in providing goods and services (not checked for websites). Target
  WCAG 2.2 AA (web-ui-design).

## Delivery, returns and trust

- State delivery times by region, including rural delivery surcharges where they apply.
- Returns page states CGA remedies plainly; any change-of-mind window as an extra.
- Trust: real reviews, NZ contact details, prices in NZD with GST.

## Selling into New Zealand from abroad

- Overseas sellers with NZ consumer sales over NZ$60,000 in 12 months register and
  collect 15% GST on goods of NZ$1,000 or less (V for the NZ$1,000 rule; R for NZ$60,000).

## Ad-policy notes

- Read the platform playbook; restricted goods (alcohol, health, gambling) apply.
- Ads quote GST-inclusive prices.

## Page checklist

- [ ] Prices include GST; delivery cost disclosed.
- [ ] Returns page states CGA remedies; no "no refunds".
- [ ] Real "was" prices; claims you can back up.
- [ ] Privacy policy covering tracking; IPP 3A notices for indirectly collected data.
- [ ] Email and SMS consent and unsubscribe.
- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA.
