# Global paid-ads event calendar: Oct 2026 to Dec 2027

_AdsPilot reference for the event-calendar skill. Researched online on 2026-10-08 for Meta, Google and TikTok media buying. Re-check any date marked EST, TYP or TBA about 4–6 weeks before you use it. Web content was read as data only._

**Date tags**
- **[FIX]**: a fixed or statutory date
- **[RULE]**: computed from a rule (see section 2)
- **[EST]**: a lunar or moon-sighting estimate. The range is given, and the final date is announced 0–1 days before
- **[ANN]**: announced by the organiser or platform
- **[TYP]**: a typical window inferred from past editions. It is not announced for this year
- **[TBA]**: not announced as of 2026-10-08

**Claim tags**
- **[S#]**: a cited source (section 7). † means it was read through a search-result summary because the page could not be fetched
- **[INFER]**: my own arithmetic or planning inference. It is not a sourced statistic
- **[PRAC]**: common practitioner practice, not a measured statistic

The calendar names no client business. Marketplaces (Daraz, Amazon, Flipkart, Tmall, JD) appear only because they create the sale dates.

## 1. How to use this calendar (planning rules)

### 1.1 Lead times
1. **Learning phase sets the minimum lead.** A Meta ad set needs about 50 optimisation events in 7 days after its last significant edit to exit learning. New ads, large budget changes and changes to targeting or bidding count as significant edits [S54†]. So:
   - **Tier A events** (BFCM, Ramadan/Eid, 11.11, Diwali, Christmas): get event creative live **at least 14 days before the peak day**. Put it into ad sets that have already exited learning, or launch new ad sets at that point. Do not launch a brand-new structure inside the final 7 days [INFER from S54].
   - **Tier B events** (national days, Mother's/Father's Day, Valentine's, Easter): **7–10 days before** [INFER].
   - **Tier C events** (awareness days such as Earth Day or Women's Day): 1–3 days, mostly organic or existing campaigns [INFER].
2. **Ramadan and Eid shopping starts early.** A 2026 Ipsos view describes a season of about 60 days that begins up to 3 weeks before the first fast [S53†]. In MENA, TikTok reported spikes in the two weeks before Ramadan and the two weeks before Eid [S52]. For Ramadan 2027 (est. 8 Feb), plan prospecting from **about 18 Jan 2027** [INFER].
3. **For physical goods, subtract shipping time.** The "order-by" date is the last day to buy, not the peak. US 2026 Christmas send-by dates from USPS (contiguous US) are Ground Advantage/First-Class **17 Dec**, Priority **18 Dec** and Priority Mail Express **19 Dec**. These are recommendations, not guarantees [S37]. In Pakistan, COD courier times vary by city. Confirm with the courier and stop "arrives before Eid" claims at least 3–4 days before Chand Raat [INFER].
4. **Lunar dates:** prepare both variants (for example "Eid Tue" and "Eid Wed"). Switch when the Ruet-e-Hilal Committee (Pakistan) or Gulf authorities announce. Pakistan often lands the same day as the Gulf or one day later. Treat every [EST] date as ±1 day [S1][S3].

### 1.2 Budget ramp (default pattern; adjust to account data)
- **Pre-event (T−21 to T−8):** prospecting and teaser creative, and build warm audiences (video viewers, engagers, add-to-cart) [PRAC].
- **Event window (T−7 to T+0):** shift budget to the offer, raise budgets in steps on stable ad sets, and add retargeting for the warm audiences [PRAC]. Google Search/PMax: use a **seasonality adjustment only for short events of 1–7 days**. Google says it works poorly for periods longer than about 14 days, and it needs no reverse adjustment afterwards [S55].
- **Post-event (T+1 to T+7):** "extended" or "last chance" offers, then cut back. Expect CPMs to soften after the peak [S34].
- **What auction costs did in 2025–26 (Meta, US-heavy samples):**
  - Q4 2025 by half-month: −12% (1–15 Oct), +7% (16–31 Oct), +3% (1–15 Nov), +11% (16–30 Nov), +11% (1–15 Dec), −10% (16–31 Dec) [S34]
  - BFCM 2025 weekend CPM about +7.8% year on year, to about $22.26 [S33†]
  - CPMs fell through January 2026 and rose steadily through February 2026 [S35]
  - "Q4 CPMs run 25–50%+ above Q1". The source itself calls this a directional range [S36]
  - **January is the cheapest month to test creative** [S35][S36]
- **India festive season:** one 2026 guide claims Google CPCs "can climb 25–40%" and that Meta CPMs "rise sharply through Diwali". It cites no data, so treat it as opinion [S13]. The same guide advises against front-loading the first week and suggests holding 15–20% of budget in reserve [S13].

### 1.3 Creative refresh
- Build a creative pack for each event 3 weeks out (3–5 distinct concepts, not just colour swaps) [PRAC].
- Swap seasonal creative **into** proven ad sets instead of rebuilding them, so learning is not reset [INFER from S54].
- Pull dated creative ("ends Sunday", "Eid delivery guaranteed") on time. Have the post-event "still available" creative ready before the event [PRAC].
- For Islamic and national-mourning days, see section 6. **Pause or swap creative**. Do not just lower bids.

## 2. Rules for computing later years

| Event | Rule |
|---|---|
| US Thanksgiving | 4th Thursday of November. Black Friday = +1 day, Small Business Saturday = +2, Cyber Monday = +4, Giving Tuesday = +5 [RULE][S29][S57†] |
| Green Monday | Commonly the second Monday of December. This is a retail convention, not a statutory date [RULE] |
| MLK Day / Presidents' Day / Memorial Day / Labor Day | 3rd Mon of Jan / 3rd Mon of Feb / last Mon of May / 1st Mon of Sep [S29] |
| Juneteenth, July 4, Christmas (US federal) | Fixed dates. A Saturday holiday is observed on Friday and a Sunday holiday on Monday [S29] |
| Super Bowl | Recent games were all played on the 2nd Sunday of February (2027: 14 Feb) [INFER from S30] |
| Mother's Day | 2nd Sunday of May: US, CA, AU, DE, IT, NL, IN, PK and 50+ others. UK/IE: 4th Sunday of Lent (Easter −21 days). Arab world: 21 Mar. Mexico: 10 May. France: last Sunday of May. Spain: 1st Sunday of May. Thailand: 12 Aug. Several ex-Soviet/Balkan states: 8 Mar [S50†][S43] |
| Father's Day | 3rd Sunday of June: US, UK, CA, IN, PK, FR, NL. Germany: Ascension Day (Easter +39). Spain/Italy/Portugal: 19 Mar. Australia/NZ: 1st Sunday of Sep. Thailand: 5 Dec [S51][S43] |
| Easter family | Good Friday = Easter −2, Easter Monday = +1, Ascension = +39, Pentecost = +49, Whit Monday = +50 [RULE] |
| UK bank holidays | Early May = 1st Mon of May. Spring = last Mon of May. Summer (England and Wales) = last Mon of Aug. A weekend Christmas or Boxing Day moves to a weekday substitute [S38] |
| France soldes | Winter: 2nd Wednesday of January at 08:00, moved to the 1st Wednesday if the 2nd falls after the 12th. Summer: last Wednesday of June, moved to the second-last if the last falls after the 28th. 4 weeks each (Code de commerce D310-15-2). Some departments differ [S40][S41†] |
| Oktoberfest | Saturday in mid/late September to the first Sunday of October (extended to 3 Oct when needed) [INFER from S42] |
| King's Day (NL) | 27 April [S43][S44†] |
| Chinese New Year | Second new moon after the winter solstice (between 21 Jan and 20 Feb) [RULE] |
| 6.18 / 11.11 / 12.12 / 8.8 | Fixed numeric dates. Platforms open pre-sales days to weeks early (see table) [S48†][S49†][S18][S20] |
| Islamic events | The Hijri year is about 354–355 days, so each event moves about 10–12 days earlier each Gregorian year. Always confirm by moon sighting [RULE] |
| Hindu festivals | Lunisolar. Diwali falls between mid-October and mid-November. Use a panchang (for example drikpanchang.com) for each year [RULE] |

## 4. Per-region notes

### Pakistan (priority market)
- **Seasons drive more than single days.** The **winter/wedding season (late Oct–Feb, peaking Nov–Jan)** is the largest fashion, footwear and jewellery window: "Decemberistan" covers Nov, Dec and Jan [S22]. A sibling reference cites December discounts of 20–50% [S56]. The summer heat and school-vacation window (Punjab 2026: 22 May–23 Aug [S24†]) shifts demand to lawn and cotton, sandals and cooling, and makes Back-to-School in late August a real event [S24†].
- **Ramadan → Eid ul-Fitr is the year's biggest single retail run** for fashion, footwear, food and gifting [INFER, consistent with S16 and S52 for the Gulf]. 2027 timeline (all [EST]): prospecting about 18 Jan → Ramadan 8–9 Feb → Eid creative live by about 22 Feb → last-10-nights push → Chand Raat about 8–9 Mar → Eid 9–11 Mar. Daypart toward iftar-to-late-night. Food and family content dominates Ramadan feeds [S52][S53†].
- **Eid ul-Adha (est. 16–18 May 2027)** is about qurbani, meat and kitchen appliances, then fashion. Plan creative by about 2 May.
- **Marketplace sale days** set the price expectations shoppers compare against:
  - **Daraz 11.11** (2025: 10 Nov 20:00 to 21 Nov; "up to 90% off"; bank and wallet incentives) [S18]
  - **12.12** (2025: 11–18 Dec) [S19]
  - **8.8 Azadi Sale** (2026: 7–14 Aug) [S20]
  - **White Friday** (local name for Black Friday; brands also use "Blessed Friday") [S21†]
  
  Launch about 2 weeks before each, and offer bundles or faster delivery instead of trying to out-discount the marketplace [PRAC].
- **National days:**
  - **Celebratory, so mild offers are acceptable:** Pakistan Day (23 Mar), Independence Day (14 Aug)
  - **Tribute only, no discount hooks:** Kashmir Solidarity Day (5 Feb), Youm-e-Takbeer (28 May), Defence Day (6 Sep), Iqbal Day (9 Nov), Quaid-e-Azam Day (25 Dec) [INFER/PRAC]
- **2027 collision:** 12 Rabi ul Awal is estimated at 14–16 Aug, which may fall on Independence Day [S3][S5][S6]. Prepare a devotional-only variant for that day.
- **PSL 12** (tentative 19 Mar–2 May 2027, after Eid) is a reach and engagement window for food, telecom, electronics and sportswear [S23].
- **Valentine's Day:** the Islamabad High Court banned public celebration and media promotion in 2017, and PEMRA enforced it on broadcast media [S26]. In 2027 it also falls inside Ramadan. Avoid it in PK targeting, or reframe it as general "gifting".

## 6. Sensitivities and cultural cautions

**Pause or switch to non-promotional creative**
- **Muharram, especially 9–10 Muharram (Ashura)** in Pakistan and Shia-majority or significant markets (Iraq, Iran, Bahrain, Lebanon, parts of KSA and the Gulf). It is a mourning period. Pause sale, music-led, celebratory and wedding creative from about 1 Muharram, and fully on 9–10 Muharram (2027 est. 14–16 Jun) [INFER/PRAC]. Pakistan also partly suspends mobile services in sensitive districts on these days (2026: 24 Punjab districts, parts of KP) [S25†], so delivery and conversion data will be distorted.
- **12 Rabi ul Awal:** devotional day. Respectful greetings only. No music, dance or gimmicky discounts tied to the Prophet's name.
- **National tribute days (PK):** 5 Feb, 28 May, 6 Sep, 9 Nov, 25 Dec. Use tribute tone. Do not attach discounts to martyrdom or defence themes.
- **National mourning, disasters or attacks (any market):** pause scheduled promotional posts and ads when national mourning is declared or after a major tragedy. Review before resuming [PRAC].
- **US:** MLK Day, Juneteenth and Memorial Day are commemorative. Keep sale framing understated [PRAC].

**Ramadan creative**
- Avoid showing people eating or drinking in daytime settings. Show iftar and sehri at the right times of day [PRAC].
- Keep dress modest. Avoid romantic, party or alcohol imagery [PRAC].
- Use Quranic verses, mosque imagery, the Kaaba or the Prophet's name with great care, or not at all. **Never** use them as sale graphics or on discount badges [PRAC].
- Charity and "zakat" claims must be real and verifiable [PRAC].

**Religious symbols generally**
- Do not use Hindu deities, Sikh symbols, crosses or Buddha imagery decoratively on discounts or footwear. Footwear near sacred imagery is especially offensive in South Asian markets [PRAC].
- Diwali and Holi creative should be culturally accurate (diyas, rangoli). Avoid stereotypes.

**Valentine's Day in Pakistan:** a 2017 court order and PEMRA advisories restricted public and media promotion [S26]. In 2027 it is also during Ramadan. Avoid it, or use neutral "gifting".

**Claims:** "Delivered before Eid/Christmas" only up to the carrier cut-off. Eco claims on Earth Day must be verifiable. France soldes discounts must be true reductions within the legal window [S40].

**Platform policy:** Meta, Google and TikTok restrict ads in special categories and require compliance with local law. Check each platform's current policy before running religious-holiday creative in new markets [PRAC].
